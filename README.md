# Noitis™

> **Technology people can trust.**

Noitis is a technology brand being built in Greece with an international horizon. We create focused software for difficult operational problems where clarity, control, and trustworthy decisions matter.

Our aim is simple: make advanced technology feel dependable. We look for places where people and businesses lose time, control, or confidence because important work is fragmented across tools and manual processes, then engineer a clearer system around the real decision.

## What we build

Noitis is building a family of focused products. Each product addresses a different problem, but all share the same standard: intelligence should be understandable, controlled, and useful in the real workflow.

### AgentGate

**AI governance** — a governance gateway for AI agents that applies organization policy and routes sensitive actions through human approval when required.

### AutoPaylot

**Financial obligations** — financial-obligation control for organizing recurring commitments and deciding what is ready before money moves.

### Business Resource Scheduler

**Operational scheduling** — constraint-based scheduling for work that depends on people, skills, equipment, spaces, locations, availability, and business rules.

### EarnLogic

**Compensation intelligence** — commercial performance and incentive compensation connected by calculation lineage that explains monetary results.

### FamilyOS

**Family coordination** — a parent-first, child-centred experience for discovery, bookings, schedules, saved items, and everyday family coordination.

### LegacyCI

**Database change intelligence** — dependency, lineage, and pre-change impact analysis for complex database systems with explicit evidence and visible uncertainty.

Our products are in active development. Public descriptions are intentionally conservative: current capability is kept separate from future work, and products without an approved public deployment are not linked to private, local, or placeholder environments.

## How we build

**Trust first.** Security, transparency, reliability, and user control are part of the product—not finishing touches.

**Useful intelligence.** Advanced technology should remove real work and uncertainty, not create complexity for its own sake.

**Human control.** Automation should expand capability while keeping important decisions understandable and accountable.

**Built for real people.** Readable, predictable interfaces should work for people of different ages and technical experience.

## Our roots

Noitis is inspired by the Greek intellectual tradition around *nous* and *noesis*: mind, thought, understanding, and the conception of an idea.

For us, Noitis is the one who sees what could exist before it exists—and engineers it into reality. That becomes a practical standard: technology should help people understand better, decide with confidence, and act with greater control.

Noitis began with a builder's question: where are people and businesses still accepting friction, uncertainty, or unnecessary manual work simply because a better system has not been built yet?
